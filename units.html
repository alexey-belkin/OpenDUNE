How to read the stats
  • Cost and Build time are the engine's ObjectInfo values. Build time is not seconds.
  • Range is the engine's fireDistance value.
  • Damage is the listed unit or projectile's damage value. A unit can launch a projectile with its own damage value.
  • Rate of fire and Est. DPS assume normal speed (120 game ticks per second), sustained firing, a full-health unit, every shot hitting, and no travel time or target-specific effects. The engine adds a negligible 0–1 tick random variation.
  • For a normal weapon, rate = 60 / fireDelay shots/s. For a two-shot weapon, rate = 240 / (2 × fireDelay + 5) shots/s; the five-tick delay is the short gap between paired shots. DPS is rate × direct damage.
  • † Special effects: Deviator damage is zero because its gas only applies deviation. Sonic waves damage each crossed tile in pulses, so a single target's DPS depends on the path and cannot be represented by one honest fixed value.
House availability

House access is transcribed from the engine's availability flags. All means Harkonnen, Atreides, Ordos, Fremen, Sardaukar, and Mercenary. Internal weapon types are not buildable units even when their house mask is All.
